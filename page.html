Frequently Asked Questions

How do you verify the Halal status of a workplace?

Our verification team conducts on-site or virtual audits focusing on financial ethics, facility management, and HR policies to ensure they align with universal Sharia standards.

Are there any membership fees for job seekers?

Standard membership for job seekers is completely free. We offer premium verification services for those wishing to fast-track their profile visibility.

Can non-Muslims use HalalHire?

Absolutely. HalalHire is an ethical platform open to all individuals who share our values of transparency, fairness, and mutual respect in the workplace.
